Engagement

Steam store page Game page

Short description

One capital ship. Fifteen minutes until relief. Bolt salvaged guns onto your hull, raise a fleet of named escorts who rank up and branch, and hold the lane against a Hollow swarm that grows every minute you survive.

Description

You command a single Concord capital ship in the Belt of Ashes. Everything you destroy drops salvage, and every level bolts something onto your hull: flak batteries, rail cannons, tesla coils, drone hangars, shield domes. Each one is a real turret on a real hardpoint. By minute ten your ship looks like the build you chose, and it fights like it.

You do not fight alone. Recruit escorts, send them where you need them, and promote the ones who live. A Lance that survives long enough takes a specialist commission and comes back with a new gun. A pilot who does not goes on the memorial wall with their rank, callsign, branch and kills.

The Hollow do not negotiate. They come in swarms, then in capital ships, then in things the Signals Office has stopped trying to name. You will see them coming: every charged beam, arcing pulse and incoming torpedo is marked on screen and in your ears before it lands.

There are no "+8% damage" cards. A refit might blow armour plates off into a flak burst when your hull is hit, relaunch a lost fighter wing, or turn your first volley into guaranteed critical hits. Your drydock, your mastery and your doctrines work the same way: mechanics you can see, not numbers you have to trust.

Features

  • Six flagships, from a line cruiser to a carrier to a cloaking raider, each with its own hardpoint layout, signature system and tree of seven refits.
  • 25 modules and 15 evolutions. Max a module, pair it with the right partner, and it becomes something else.
  • A fleet that grows with you. Nine escort classes to recruit, order, promote and branch. Pilots keep their callsigns, ranks and records.
  • 21 Hollow ship types across five sectors, each sector with its own hazard: ice shard storms, a collapsing dead shipyard, a lightning nebula that halves your sensors, and Hollow space itself.
  • Bosses with phases: the Reaver dreadnought, the Maw that swallows escorts whole, the Choir that sings its dead back, and the Hollow Crown.
  • Campaign: three acts on a branching map where your hull, modules and pilots carry forward between fights. Eight keepers run the shops, from a salvage broker to a mercenary post to a chapel that keeps the lost, plus a dealer in Hollow-tech the Concord would rather you did not meet.
  • A service record that lasts. Forty Command ranks, mastery for every flagship with perks of its own, nine doctrines that trade a strength for a weakness, a drydock of seventeen upgrades, and weekly commissions.
  • Endless, Daily, Boss Rush and Challenge modes, and a Heat ladder for when fifteen minutes stops being enough.
  • A launch you will want to watch: every sortie opens with your ship leaving its yard cradle and burning out of the system.
  • Chase or bridge camera, photo mode, full controller support with Steam Deck button prompts, and 70 Steam achievements.
  • Accessibility: colourblind palettes (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, high contrast), UI and HUD scale, reduced motion, reduced flashes, hold or toggle for boost and the orders wheel, remappable keys, aim assist, adjustable damage taken, and radio chatter shown on screen.

FIGHT

Steam store page Game page

Short description

Let's FIGHT. Queue your steps, guards, and strikes, then commit blind and watch both plans collide into skill-based warfare. Master over twenty weapon-bound fighters, posture breaks, parries and projectiles, with real-time replays to watch the battle back in style.

Description

FIGHT is a tactical fighting game about hidden plans. Queue movement, guards, strikes, and special attacks on a shared timeline. Commit before you know the opposing plan. Then watch the two sequences meet in real time. FIGHT rewards choices made before impact. Close the distance with aggressive strikes, hold ground with guards and parries, create space for a projectile, or use movement to bait an answer.

Choose from over twenty weapon-led fighters. Each move set changes the questions you ask: who controls distance, who threatens a guard, and which attack creates space for the next plan. Learn what every fighter can represent before both players lock in.

Every action has a place on the timeline. Build a chain of movement, defense, strikes, and specials, then commit blind. You do not see the opposing sequence until both plans resolve. Damage matters, but posture creates the opening: pressure a guarded opponent, force a response, and turn a broken defense into a punish.

Once both players commit, the fight runs in real time. Watch the collision, pause on the decision that changed the exchange, and replay the sequence to see how a guard, parry, strike, projectile, or repositioning determined the result.

Genres

Street Takeover

Steam store page Game page

Short description

Drive Fast. Pit Harder. Takeover. Street Takeover is a racing game about high-speed police chases, wrecked and damaged car physics, and taking on the county from the racer's seat or leading its police force in cleaning up the town.

Description

Street Takeover is a racing game built around crashes that actually matter.

Hit a wall and the car takes the hit. Lose a bumper and it stays gone. Bend a wheel and you will feel it through the next corner. Skip the repair bill and take the damage into the next race. If the car gets written off, it is gone.

And the police are learning while you are learning.

Play the getaway campaign and try to stay ahead of a county-wide police response that changes based on what you have already done.

Start with one cruiser on a back road. Keep causing trouble and the response gets heavier: more units, unmarked cars, spike strips, roadblocks, bridge blocks, and eventually the state detail.

Then play those same ten nights from the other side.

As police, you box runners in, call roadblocks, deploy spikes, and try to bring them home without wrecking your own cars. The two campaigns share the same roads, the same events, and the same county.

Features

  • Persistent vehicle damage: bodywork, wheels, suspension, glass, engine and radiator damage all affect how the car drives.
  • Single-player campaigns for both getaway racers and police
  • Split-screen racing and online multiplayer
  • Free roam, time trial, drift, duel, derby, elimination and points events
  • Keyboard, gamepad and wheel support with rebindable controls
  • Photo mode
  • Driving school

Genres

  • Indie
  • Massively Multiplayer
  • Racing

Field Surgeon

Steam store page Game page

Short description

Scrub in as the field surgeon of a city at war. Draw arrows from bone, close sabre cuts, cut soldiers out of their armour and saw off what cannot be saved, on humans, dwarves, elves, orcs, hornfolk and giants. Then face eight plagues a heretic choir has sung into your patients.

Description

Plague smoulders in the Tanners' Rows, the Ostrau company is fighting at the city's gates, and the Hospice of Saint Ildra takes in everyone the city throws away. You are its newly sworn apothecary-surgeon, and the table is never empty.

Eight period instruments: lancet, tongs, leech-pipe, gut thread, Saint's salve, tincture, cautery brand and scrying lens, each with its own motion. Ease an arrow through the muscle that grips it, rock a head loose from the bone, close a sabre cut muscle first and skin second, soften boiling pitch before you lift it, wind a tourniquet before you look for the bleeding vessel, and seal a sucking chest wound as the patient breathes out. Every action is graded; clean chains raise your rank.

Six races to learn. Humans, dwarves, elves, orcs, hornfolk and giants all come through the Hospice doors, and no two cut, bleed or heal alike. A dwarf's hide is thick and his blood dark and slow; the lancet must work patiently. An elf's skin is so thin the lamp glows through it: easy to open, quick to bleed, and unforgiving of a slip. An orc's leather hide turns a hasty blade, his near-black blood clots fast, and tinctures barely touch him. A giant bleeds out slowly and heals slower still. Know your patient before you cut.

Draw the five-pointed sign to invoke the Litany of Stillness and slow time for a few precious seconds, once per operation. Careful: if the Inquisitor of the Ash Tribunal sees it, he will condemn you for witchcraft.

A heretic coven, the Hollow Choir, sings to the Mother of Plagues, and each of its hymns takes root in a host as a disease pushed past anything nature allows: the Vigil Plague, the Answering Pox, the Red Roll, Saint Ildra's Fire and four more. Each one flares in time with its hymn and its bell. Learn the rhythm, then cut it out. Every host you save, or lose, leaves its mark on the city.

Features

  • Five chapters, over sixty operations, eight Malison Hours: a surgery-action campaign in a grimdark early-modern world
  • Injuries surgeons really faced: arrows, sabre cuts, crushing mace blows, boiling pitch, severed arteries, amputations
  • Eight period instruments with precise mouse-and-keyboard controls, controller and Steam Deck support
  • Graded, combo-driven operations with ranks to chase in every case, plus Guild Trials and Challenge operations
  • Assists and accessibility options: generous timers, simplified gestures, one-handed play, a Litany key, reduced flashing and gore settings

Genres

  • Indie
  • RPG
  • Simulation

MOTHERSHIP: The Last Stand

Steam store page Game page

Short description

Take command of Mothership Keystone, the last fortress-station of the Compact: aim its Prime Battery, build defenses under fire and break flagship after flagship across a war of 24 systems. Every run makes your Admiralty stronger.

Description

The Kharon Ascendancy came through the gate with carriers, siege titans and the Worldship itself. Between them and the Reach stands Mothership Keystone: a shipyard finished as a fortress, eleven thousand crew on its habitat ring and, on its crown, the Prime Battery, the largest gun the Compact ever mounted. You are in command.

Lead fast raiders, crack armored hulls and switch between six primary weapon families, each with its own rhythm: the Pulse Driver, Rail Lance, Flak Thrower, Helion Beam, Ion Repeater and Storm Array. Back them with secondaries from the Siege Torpedo to the Orbital Barrage, and reforge every barrel in the Foundry until no Kharon captain survives it twice.

Drop into build mode and time slows to a crawl. Socket 13 structures onto Keystone's hull and habitat ring (flak batteries, rail turrets, Tesla spires, point defense grids, hangars and bomber bays) and upgrade them to Mk III while the next wave jumps in. Turn the battle with 10 command abilities, from Chrono Field and Shield Surge to Orbital Barrage and EMP Nova.

Twenty-three Kharon hulls, from Skimmers that rake the guns to Splinters that break into Slivers, lead up to four flagships: the Tyrant dreadnought, the Maw, the Sunbreaker siege titan and the Worldship. Tear off their batteries, shield generators, launch bays and heat vents one by one.

Keystone cannot be everywhere. Each cycle you choose where to jump: defend a system, break a siege, escort a convoy, hold out or take on a flagship, while the worlds you cannot reach fall back. Between battles, spend on 79 research nodes, hire and train named wings, trade at the Exchange and live with the events of a long war.

Every run earns Command XP. Invest it in 32 standing orders across Logistics, Engineering, Gunnery and Fleet, then climb 10 Ascension levels for a war the Kharon fight harder every time.

Features

  • A full campaign across 24 systems in 3 sectors, plus Skirmish, Endless Siege and Training
  • 6 mission types and 4 difficulties, from Cadet to Ironclad
  • 6 primary and 6 secondary weapons, 13 structures, 10 abilities, 79 research nodes
  • 23 Kharon hulls and 4 multi-part flagships
  • A persistent Admiralty talent tree and 10 Ascension levels
  • Voiced battle callouts, full controller support, rebindable controls and accessibility options

Phantasm Catalyst

Steam store page Game page

Short description

A tactical anime roguelike deckbuilder. Read every foe's countdown, outplay them with a shared hand of skills, and sail the Ark through four broken realms and the Rift to stop the hunger that eats time.

Description

The world broke when the Chronovore came, a hunger that eats time. What was left drifts apart in the Rift as Phantasm: realms that only remember themselves. Sora, the Ark's last navigator, can still make a phantasm real again, but only with a crew.

Choose two of sixteen heroes, each from a different broken realm, and lead them through salt deserts, lantern cities, ink forests and storm-wracked seas. Every battle is a puzzle you can read: every foe shows what it will do and when, and every skill you cast ticks their countdowns down. Spend your mana, weigh Swift skills against Overload, wait for the right moment, and strike before they do.

When a hero refuses to give up, their power breaks loose: Awakenings that rewrite their skills, Apex forms, Worldseals that bend the battlefield to one person's will, and Fracture Strikes that crack the air itself, each with its own animated cut-in. The Sovereigns who rule each realm rise to meet you, and transform before your eyes when you break them.

Between battles, choose your road: fights, elites, events, camps, shops and treasure. Train your crew, forge and enchant your gear, recruit new crewmates, and grow closer around the fire. Lose, and the Ark remembers: every voyage earns Ark Memories that unlock heroes, gear and Sora's charts.

Features

  • Tactics you can read: every foe shows its next move and its countdown. Share one hand of skills across the crew, push your luck with Overload, take free tempo with Swift skills, or Wait for the perfect moment. Heroes feel pain, recover, and fight on at Death's Door.
  • Sixteen original heroes with their own kits, forms, ultimates, Worldseals, friendships and story arcs, plus Sora, the navigator who fights beside you.
  • Four realms and the Rift, each with its own foes, battlefields, events and Sovereigns.
  • A full voyage in about an hour, with endless voyages, twenty ascensions, mutators, challenges and a daily voyage with its own seed.
  • Shareable run codes: send a friend the exact voyage you just played.
  • Animated anime presentation: ultimate cut-ins, Sovereign entrances and transformations, living painted battlefields, hand-animated spell effects and a sweeping Japanese-inspired soundtrack.
  • Story, Normal and Expert difficulties and a full set of accessibility options, including reduced motion and a photosensitivity mode.

Ace Academy

Steam store page Game page

Short description

At Ace Academy, rank is won at the table and every debt is written in the Ledger. Gamble, read faces, raise the stakes, catch cheats in the act and cheat back if you dare. Climb a ladder of academy rivals through seven chapters of high-stakes anime drama, or end up expelled.

Description

Tsukasa Kurenai has just transferred to Ace Academy, a private college where standing is decided at the gambling table. The student council, the House, holds every student's debts in the Ledger. Win, and you rise. Lose too much, and you risk expulsion.

Every opponent has a face that tells, habits they cannot help and a cheat they think you will never notice. Watch for the twitch, the pause, the change of voice. Keep notes in your Notebook. Raise when they are bluffing, fold when they are not, and push them all in when the moment is right.

When something feels wrong, call it. Name the cheat correctly and they pay double. Get it wrong and you pay instead. Or set a trap, let them cheat into it, and watch their own trick turn against them.

Peek, palm, a loaded die, a mirror at just the right angle. Your own tricks can win a hand outright, but every opponent is watching too, and getting caught has a price.

Blind Card, Twenty-One Duel, Liar's Dice, Sealed Bid, Knife and Ribbon and more: fourteen games of nerve, each with a Highfold twist. Each rival plays their own signature game, from the Library Committee Chair's dice to the Head of the House's gauntlet of every game you have survived so far.

Seven chapters, two routes that split when the House makes you an offer, and six endings, plus the one where the Ledger takes your name. What you take from each defeated rival, what you forgive and who you stake it all for decides how the story ends.

Features

  • A full story campaign across seven chapters and two routes, with twenty opponents, each with their own game, tells, habits and cheat
  • Fourteen gambling games, from card duels and dice to sealed bids and counting games
  • Read tells, call out cheats by name, set traps and use tricks of your own
  • Thrill and Frenzy: push your luck for perfect reads
  • The Ledger: borrow to stay at the table, pay it back or sink into debt
  • Two story routes and six endings, shaped by the choices you make between tables
  • Rematches and practice tables for every rival you have beaten
  • Keyboard and mouse play, reduced motion, a four-colour deck and adjustable text size

SLINGHOOK

Steam store page Game page

Short description

Fire your slinghook and fly. Tap to yank yourself toward any hook point, hold to swing, and chain it all without touching the ground. Race through eight photoreal landscapes as fast as you can. No enemies, no combat, just speed.

Description

Slinghook is a game about one thing: moving fast through beautiful places.

You have a grappling hook and two moves. Tap to yank yourself toward whatever you hit: a giant tree, a sandstone spire, a sea stack, a floating island. Hold to swing, then let go at the right moment to launch. Time a release just past the bottom of the arc for a perfect boost. Chain hooks without landing to build flow. Roll on landing to keep your speed. Skim across water, ride updrafts and snap through boost rings.

There's no combat and nothing to fight. It's just you, the rope, and the landscape.

Features

  • Verdant Giants: an ancient forest of colossal trees
  • Sunspire Canyon: a sandstone canyon of towering spires
  • Drifting Isles: floating islands over open meadows
  • Frostveil Peaks: snow ridges and ice crystals that snap if you hang on too long
  • Tidecliff Coast: sea stacks rising from the surf
  • Ember Wilds: an autumn forest at golden hour
  • Glasswater Marsh: still pools and drowned woods
  • Stormcrown Highlands: terraced plateaus under heavy skies

Genres

  • Adventure
  • Indie
