# Non-Reddit Online Communities and Platforms for Indie Game Promotion (excluding YouTube/Twitch creators)

Scope note for the report writer: hard, attributable wishlist numbers for non-Reddit community channels are rare. Most published data covers Steam festivals, streamers and Reddit, all outside this scope. Every finding below is labeled:
- **[Dev-reported]**: a developer published the number about their own game.
- **[Analyst]**: Chris Zukowski (How To Market A Game), GameDiscoverCo, or a similar marketer, usually aggregating developer reports.
- **[Anecdotal]**: qualitative, a marketing blog, or a secondary summary.

Where a figure came only from a search-engine excerpt and not from the full page, it says so.

## Which of these channels have documented wishlist or sales results (numbers, dates, genre)?

### Takeaway
Only a few non-Reddit community channels have any published numbers:
- **Imgur:** about 400 wishlists from one front-page post, but that figure is from 2021 and Imgur has declined since.
- **Niche X aggregator accounts:** a measurable lift for a free prologue in 2023.
- **A paid indie showcase:** about 65 wishlists for $600, for a roguelite in 2026.
- **Hacker News:** one large post-launch Show HN (Ballionaire, a roguelike) with no wishlist attribution.

Social posting in general is reported to yield about 20 wishlists per "viral moment". Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon, itch.io, Product Hunt, 4chan, ResetEra, TIGSource, RPGCodex, Facebook groups, LinkedIn and Tumblr had no attributable wishlist data that I could find.

### Cited Findings

**Imgur**
- [Analyst, Aug 2021] Zukowski said an earlier Imgur strategy "could yield about 400 wishlists".
  - He graphed 175+ posts from Apr 29, 2020 to Aug 28, 2021.
  - A post needs about 100 upvotes within 4 hours to reach "most viral", which typically brings 1,000+ upvotes. Posts that miss it usually stay at about 100 upvotes or fewer.
  - By mid-2021, most-viral posts rarely passed 1,500 upvotes, compared with several above 3,000 in September 2020.
  - His conclusion: Imgur is "fading as a source of cheap wishlists" and festivals now have far more potential.
  - Source: [How To Market A Game – Does Imgur still work?](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/08/30/does-imgur-still-work/)
- [Analyst, 2021] What still worked on Imgur:
  - Turning the best part of a trailer into a GIF, timed with a major beat (announcement, launch-date reveal or launch).
  - "I quit my job" posts, though weaker than before.
  - AAA-style 3D graphics performed best; pixel art "went viral in the past but is now rarer".
  - Post occasionally and spend no more than an hour per post.
  - Source: [HTMAG – Does Imgur still work?](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/08/30/does-imgur-still-work/)
- [Anecdotal] A Game Developer blog warns that a title like "Hey imgur, this is our new game" gets called out as advertising, and that front-page traffic must be converted into followers on another platform. Source: [Game Developer – How to get on the front page of Imgur](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-get-on-the-front-page-of-imgur)

**X/Twitter aggregator and showcase accounts**
- [Analyst + dev-reported, Aug 7, 2023] The Karters 2: Turbo Charged (kart racer). Source: [HTMAG – How to get wishlists using Twitter game aggregators](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/08/07/how-to-get-wishlists-using-twitter-game-aggregators/)
  - Its free prologue was tweeted twice by @SteamGamesPC (FreeSteamGamesPC, 74,000 followers) on Jul 30, 2023.
  - Those tweets, plus press, "reversed the decline" in wishlists after the prologue spike. The article gives no wishlist count for the tweets.
  - For comparison, Steam Next Fest brought about 2,000 wishlists.
  - A paid post on @HumansNoContext (4.5M followers) produced no measurable lift.
  - Developers said a tweet from CanYouPetTheDog "didn't move wishlists much".
  - Takeaway from the article: audience fit beats follower count, and aggregators amplify an existing beat (a free prologue or a festival) rather than creating a hit.

**Social media in general (X, Bluesky and similar)**
- [Analyst, interview summary, Jun 2, 2026] Zukowski: "Social media has basically never worked for indie games." When he traced viral moments back to the developers, the wishlists gained were "usually around twenty". Source: [Freaking Cool Indies – Chris Z interview](https://freakingcoolindies.com/chris-z-teaches-indie-devs-to-build-an-audience-heres-how-he-built-his/)
- [Anecdotal, 2026] Developers who post on X every day reportedly net about 30 wishlists a month. This is the article's own observation, not a dataset. Source: [Steam Page Analyzer – How to get Steam wishlists](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-to-get-steam-wishlists)
- [Anecdotal, 2022; from search excerpt only] Matt Fife: Twitter and similar social media "pretty much does not work", with almost everyone under 100 wishlists from it. Source: [mattfife.com – How to Market your Game in 2022](https://mattfife.com/?p=8386)
- [Analyst, 2021; from search excerpt only] HTMAG says nobody clicks links on Twitter, and Discord click-through is much higher, at 5%–30%. Source: [HTMAG – How to use Twitter to market your game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/02/08/how-to-use-twitter-to-market-your-game/)

**Bluesky**
- [Anecdotal, Nov 18, 2024] Game Developer surveyed developers on Bluesky. It reported no follower, click, wishlist or sales numbers. Source: [Game Developer – What are game developers getting out of Bluesky?](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/what-are-game-developers-getting-out-of-bluesky-)
  - Polygon Treehouse (Mythwrecked) reported better engagement than on other platforms.
  - Finji was setting up game accounts because of growth and engagement there.
  - Robert Yang said Bluesky does not suppress off-platform links: "Even when there were only like 5 people on Bluesky, that was already 5 more people than Twitter would let me reach."
- [Anecdotal, non-games] A Mississippi Free Press editor saw about 20x the engagement on Bluesky for identical posts compared with Twitter. Musk confirmed that X deprioritized posts with links. Source: [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/bluesky-x-becomes-social-media-rcna181685)
- [Platform fact, Mar 20, 2025] Bluesky's COO said it does not demote links, and Bluesky made referral tracking easier for publishers. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/20/bluesky-makes-it-easier-for-publishers-to-track-referrals)

**Hacker News (Show HN)**
- [Dev-reported, Feb 26, 2025] *Ballionaire* (roguelike pachinko/plinko, published by Raw Fury, launched Dec 2024). Source: [HN – Show HN: I got laid off from Meta and created a minor hit on Steam](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43186406)
  - The Show HN post reached 1,581 points and 356 comments.
  - The developer reported about 200,000 units in the first 10 weeks. The post came after launch, so it reports success rather than causing it.
  - The developer credited trailers and "sponsored streams" ("most of the paid promotion was sponsored streams") and the game's "natural tendency for organic spread".
  - No wishlist or traffic figures were given, and HN's own effect on sales was not quantified. (Only the first half of the thread was read.)
- [Dev-reported, 2018, outside date range] *The Curious Expedition* Show HN reported 130,000+ units sold, with no HN attribution. Source: [HN – Show HN: The Curious Expedition](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17080985)
- [Anecdotal] HN users note gamedev topics rarely reach the front page: "it might be months before a particular topic you are interested in like gamedev makes it to the front page". Source: [HN – Ask HN: Hacker News-like forum for game development?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40419289)

**Paid indie showcase / "indie showcase" accounts**
- [Dev-reported, Mar 11, 2026] *Monster Punk* (vehicular combat roguelite, Fooya Games, two-person team). Source: [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/indie-dev-reveals-they-got-just-65-new-wishlists-for-their-game-after-paying-for-a-slot-in-the-mix-showcase/)
  - Paid $600 for a slot in The Mix + Kinda Funny Spring Showcase.
  - Gained about 35 wishlists in the first 3 hours and about 65 in 24 hours.
  - About 30 of those came from GameTrailers' YouTube upload (1.25M subscribers), which had about 2,000–2,600 views.
  - TheGamer's break-even math: at $20, about 50% of those wishlisters would need to buy; at $10, all of them.

**itch.io**
- [Dev-reported, no attribution] A *Gamblers Table* devlog reports wishlists rising from 0 to 10,000 in three weeks, alongside an itch.io demo. It does not say how much came from itch.io. Source: [itch.io – Gamblers Table](https://greenpixels.itch.io/gamblers-table)

**Benchmarks for scale (not community channels)**
- [Analyst, GameDiscoverCo via secondary, 2026] Feb 2026 Next Fest:
  - Median demo gained about 200 wishlists; the top 5% gained about 7,000.
  - Zukowski's survey of 182 developers found a median of 322 wishlists gained for games with 0–999 wishlists before the fest.
  - He estimates online festivals plus streamer coverage drive about 90% of new wishlist adds (directional).
  - Source: [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-to-get-steam-wishlists)
- [Analyst; from search excerpt only] HTMAG estimates each festival earns about 1,000–3,000 wishlists. Source: [HTMAG – How many wishlists should I have](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/09/26/how-many-wishlists-should-i-have-when-i-launch-my-game/)
- [Analyst, Oct 15, 2026] Zukowski argues Steam has "wishlist inflation": wishlists are less predictive of sales, and the velocity of wishlist adds forecasts sales almost as well as the total. Source: [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/10/15/steam-is-experiencing-wishlist-inflation-but-they-are-still-important-analysis)
- [Dev-reported, ~Nov 2026] Publisher 2 Left Thumbs had 11,000+ wishlists but a peak of 15 concurrent players at launch. It blamed a delayed Steam approval and a "tidal wave of AI slop". Sources: [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/publisher-blames-ai-slop-on-steam-as-their-game-only-gets-15-players-at-launch-3424285/); [Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/02/indie-publisher-says-steams-ai-flooded-backlog-derailed-its-games-launch)

### Inferences
**Evidence-weighted ranking of non-Reddit, non-creator community channels for a roguelite/survivors-like** (my synthesis, best evidence first):

| Rank | Channel | Evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free indie showcases and Steam themed fests | Strongest numbers (hundreds to thousands of wishlists per event) | Not "communities" strictly, but the benchmark every other channel should be compared against. |
| 2 | Genre-fit Discord servers with promo or showcase channels | High click-through (5–30%, 2021 excerpt); no wishlist counts | Low volume, high intent. Best for playtest recruitment and demo feedback. |
| 3 | Niche X aggregator accounts (free games, demos) | Documented qualitative lift in 2023 when paired with a free prologue or demo | Follower count does not predict results. |
| 4 | Bluesky (#ScreenshotSaturday feed, starter packs, custom feeds) | Better engagement than X and no link suppression, but no wishlist numbers | Good for dev-community visibility; weak passive discovery. |
| 5 | Imgur | About 400 wishlists per front-page post in 2020–21, clearly declining | Viral GIF posts favor 3D visuals over pixel art. |
| 6 | Hacker News Show HN | Can draw huge engagement (Ballionaire, 1,581 points) but no wishlist attribution | Gamedev rarely reaches the front page. Best for technically novel games or developer-story angles. |
| 7 | X hashtags (#WishlistWednesday, #ScreenshotSaturday, #PitchYaGame) | Unmeasured | Expect tens of wishlists per viral moment. |
| 8 | itch.io demo hosting and devlogs | No attribution data | Mostly a jam-player audience. |
| 9 | Paid showcases | 65 wishlists for $600 for a roguelite | Poor ROI for small teams. |
| 10 | Product Hunt, 4chan, ResetEra, TIGSource, RPGCodex, Something Awful, NeoGAF, Facebook groups, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon, Tumblr, gaming wikis | No evidence found | Treat as unproven. |

- Because almost no non-Reddit channel has attributable numbers, the practical recommendation is to tag every community link with Steam UTM parameters and measure it directly. See Q5.

### Gaps
- No published numbers for Threads, Mastodon, Tumblr, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, X Communities, or Bluesky wishlist conversion. The searches returned only qualitative pieces or platform directories.
- No 2023–2026 Imgur data. The HTMAG piece is from 2021.
- No numbers for #ScreenshotSaturday, #WishlistWednesday or #PitchYaGame participation.
- I could not read the second half of the Ballionaire HN thread (the fetch was truncated at 100k characters). It may contain more on HN's effect.

## Which large Discord servers or community hubs accept indie self-promotion and fit roguelites/survivors-likes, and what are their rules?

### Takeaway
The best-documented promo-friendly Discords are creator-run community servers (Splattercat, Alpha Beta Gamer, Nookrium and others). Their shared rules:
- Promotion goes only in a named channel, never in general chat.
- Post links at milestones (Steam page, trailer, demo, launch), not continuously.
- Some servers restrict genre.

Roguelite-specific hubs exist; Roguelike Renegades is described as the largest generalized roguelite server on Disboard. I could not confirm their self-promotion rules.

### Cited Findings
- [Community list, Jul 23, 2025] An itch.io forum post rates creator Discords that are friendly to indie devs. Member counts are not given; "activity" ratings are. Source: [itch.io – Top content creators Discord communities friendly for indie game devs](https://itch.io/t/5118887/top-content-creators-discord-communities-friendly-for-indie-game-devs)

| Server | Activity | Dev channels | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Splattercat | Very high | #content-promotion (links at milestones), #game-dev (progress, screenshots), #gamekeys-readpin (giveaways) | No game talk in #greathall; giveaways follow pinned guidelines. |
| Alpha Beta Gamer | Very high | #show-your-games, #playtest, #roast-my-steam-page, #game-dev-chat | No game talk in #general. The author notes #show-your-games uses a forum format "few non-devs use". |
| Nookrium | Good | #share-your-stuff (milestone links; give feedback too) | Giveaways: email Nook first. |
| Perafilozof | Good | Dev channels | RTS and tactics games only. |
| Frazzz | Decent | #devs-publishers-lounge (very permissive) | — |
| Henchbots | Decent | #arts-crafts-creations | — |
| Christopher Odd | Low | #self-promotion | Big milestones only; turn-based games only. |
| Bestindiegames | Low | #upcoming-indie-games (coming-soon pages), #game-dev-promo | Any indie genre. |

- The same list states no explicit frequency limits; the only timing guidance is "post links at milestones". Source: [itch.io list](https://itch.io/t/5118887/top-content-creators-discord-communities-friendly-for-indie-game-devs)
- [Server listing] Roguelike Renegades describes itself as "the largest generalized roguelite server on disboard". It has gaming chats, announcements and game nights, and members can request game-specific channels. The listing says nothing about self-promotion. Source: [Discodus – Roguelike Renegades](https://discodus.com/server/891827837965832234)
- [Directories] Roguelite and roguelike server directories exist on [top.gg (roguelite tag)](https://top.gg/discord/servers/tag/roguelite), [Disboard (roguelite tag)](https://disboard.org/servers/tag/roguelite), [Disboard (roguelike tag)](https://disboard.org/servers/tag/roguelike), [Discord.me (roguelike)](https://discord.me/servers/tag/roguelike) and [Discadia](https://discadia.com/?q=roguelike). Disboard returned HTTP 403 to my fetch, so I could not read member counts or rules.
- [Rules norms, guides; from search excerpts] Common promo-channel rules are a dedicated channel, a frequency cap (for example, one post per week), a ban on link-only posts, and sometimes a 24-hour slowmode. Source: [discords.ai – How to set up a Discord promotion channel](https://www.discords.ai/wiki/discord-promo-channel-setup)
- [Jam servers] Game-jam Discords such as roguelike jams often have a #share-your-game channel, but it is scoped to that jam. Source: [itch.io – GameDevHQ jam topic](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevhq-community-jam-2022/topic/2316798/can-we-promote-our-game-on-the-discord-server)
- [Anecdotal, marketing blog; from search excerpt] Discord suits retention and deeper engagement with players who already know the game. One tactic is a channel reserved for people who have wishlisted. Source: [GameTyrant](https://gametyrant.com/news/social-media-as-a-wishlist-building-tool-what-works-and-what-doesnt)

### Inferences
- **Overlap with excluded scope:** the strongest documented promo Discords are run by YouTube creators. Promoting in their Discords is a community channel, not creator outreach, but it overlaps the user's excluded creator scope. It can still prime a creator to cover the game.
- **For roguelites/survivors-likes:** Splattercat's and Alpha Beta Gamer's very active servers are the documented fits. Perafilozof (RTS/tactics only) and Christopher Odd (turn-based only) are probably poor fits unless the game matches their genres.
- **Etiquette:** "milestone-only" posting plus "give feedback to others" is the shared norm. Breaking it is the main ban risk.

### Gaps
- No verified member counts or written self-promo rules for large roguelite servers (Roguelike Renegades, the r/roguelites or r/roguelikes Discords, survivors-like communities). The directory pages were blocked or did not list rules.
- No wishlist data attributed to any specific Discord server.
- I found no evidence about general indie dev Discords (for example, Game Dev League or GDN) as promotion venues. Those communities are mostly developers, not players.

## Is there evidence that Hacker News, Product Hunt, 4chan, ResetEra or Imgur posts drove wishlists for indie games?

### Takeaway
- **Imgur:** the only one with a published wishlist figure, about 400 per front-page post in 2020–21, now declining.
- **Hacker News:** large Show HN threads for games that already sold well (Ballionaire, about 200k units), but no developer-reported wishlist attribution.
- **Product Hunt, 4chan and ResetEra:** I found no documented evidence of driving wishlists.

### Cited Findings
- **Imgur:** about 400 wishlists from an earlier front-page strategy; fading by 2021. Source: [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/08/30/does-imgur-still-work/)
- **Hacker News:**
  - Ballionaire's Show HN (Feb 26, 2025) reached 1,581 points and 356 comments, about 10 weeks after a launch that sold about 200k units. No wishlist or sales attribution to HN. Source: [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43186406)
  - HN has dedicated indie-game discovery spinoffs, a "Show HN: Weekly newsletter of the best indie games from Hacker News" and a "Show HN: The HN Arcade", which suggests a niche HN audience for indie games. Neither reports wishlist data. Sources: [HN newsletter](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47002446); [HN Arcade](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46793693)
  - A developer used Show HN to promote a browser-playable build ("Show HN: Play the game I'm developing directly on its website", Athena Crisis, 2024). The excerpt gave no numbers. Source: [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39365135)
- **Product Hunt:** a search for Product Hunt game launches with Steam wishlist outcomes found only a Product Hunt listing for a wishlist-tracking tool, not a game launch case study. Source: [Product Hunt – Steam Wishlist Pulse](https://www.producthunt.com/products/steam-wishlist-pulse)
- **4chan:**
  - No source tied /v/ or /vg/ /agdg/ threads to a Steam wishlist or sales spike. The closest item is a 2014 crowdfunding story (The Fine Young Capitalists), from an informal Medium post. Source: [Medium](https://medium.com/@TurelCaccese/the-sad-story-of-the-biggest-game-ever-made-on-4chan-5daf7f673387)
  - /agdg/ is a developer-resource community, not a player audience. Source: [AGDG Links](https://alloyed.github.io/agdg-links/)
- **ResetEra:** the search returned no ResetEra pages on indie self-promotion rules or results.

### Inferences
- **HN and Product Hunt:** their audiences are developers and techies, so they likely work best for games with a technical hook (custom engine, novel simulation, solo-dev story) or browser-playable builds. A typical roguelite or survivors-like has no such hook. This is an inference; no data supports or refutes it.
- **Absence of evidence:** 4chan, ResetEra and Product Hunt postmortems may exist but are not indexed or published. Treat these channels as unproven, not as disproven.

### Gaps
- No developer-reported wishlist deltas from a Show HN post (pre-launch).
- No Product Hunt game-launch case study.
- No 4chan, ResetEra, TIGSource, Something Awful, NeoGAF or RPGCodex case studies. Archive sites (4plebs, desuarchive) were not searchable with my tools.
- No 2023–2026 Imgur data.

## Are there weekly recurring promo events (e.g. #WishlistWednesday, Indie Sunday, wholesome showcases) worth joining?

### Takeaway
Several recurring opportunities exist:
- #ScreenshotSaturday, which has a dedicated Bluesky custom feed.
- #WishlistWednesday on X.
- Annual or seasonal hashtag showcases (#PitchYaGame).
- Bluesky starter packs.
- A pipeline of mostly free showcase submissions and Valve themed fests.

None of the hashtag events has published wishlist results. The free showcases and fests are the recurring events with the best evidence behind them.

### Cited Findings
- **#ScreenshotSaturday on Bluesky:** a custom feed by @trezy.codes. Opt in with #ScreenshotSaturday; opt out with #Private, #NoFeed or #NoScreenshotSaturday. Sources: [Bluesky – Screenshot Saturday feed](https://bsky.app/profile/trezy.codes/feed/screenshot-sat); [Chequered Ink – Bluesky feeds for game devs](https://chequered.ink/bluesky-feeds-for-game-devs/)
- **Bluesky Starter Packs:** examples include "Devs with Steam Next Fest Demos 2025" and "Indie Studios that launched games in 2025" (the latter requires a store link to join). These lists curate accounts and report no outcomes. The directory site's dates are inconsistent. Source: [blueskystarterpack.com – Indie dev](https://blueskystarterpack.com/indie-dev)
- **#WishlistWednesday on X:** a weekly event where devs post trailers, demos and Steam links. Its effect is visible only through likes, retweets and comments; no measured wishlist change has been published. The source is a wiki-style aggregator, so treat it cautiously. Sources: [Grokipedia – Wishlist Wednesday](https://grokipedia.com/page/Wishlist_Wednesday). Indie Sagas runs a curated "Wishlist Wednesday" column: [Indie Sagas](https://indiesagas.com/news-and-blog/wishlist-wednesday-indie-games/)
- **#PitchYaGame:**
  - A hashtag pitch showcase on X. In its 2021 round, organizer Liam Twose estimated 1,100–1,300 games were pitched. He describes it as a showcase rather than a hardcore competition. Sources: [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/pitchyagame-contest-saw-thousands-of-devs-pitch-some-incredible-looking-games/1100-6497671/); [Wikipedia – PitchYaGame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PitchYaGame)
  - IndieGameLover publishes standout roundups. Source: [IndieGameLover](https://www.indiegamelover.com/PitchYaGamesIndieGamePitches1)
  - No wishlist results were published.
- **Showcase and fest pipeline (as of Nov 2026):** Source: [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-to-get-steam-wishlists)
  - Zukowski's showcase directory (Nov 2, 2026) listed ten open calls, seven of them free.
  - Wholesome Snack 2026 was free (closed Sep 15).
  - The MIX fall event costs $600 (closed Aug 10).
  - Upcoming Valve themed fests: Auto-Battler RPG Fest (Nov 16–23, 2026), Shop Keeper Fest (Jan 25–Feb 1, 2027), Couch Co-Op Fest (Feb 8–15, 2027).
- **Paid showcase ROI caution:** $600 bought about 65 wishlists for a roguelite (Monster Punk, 2026). Source: [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/indie-dev-reveals-they-got-just-65-new-wishlists-for-their-game-after-paying-for-a-slot-in-the-mix-showcase/)

### Inferences
- **Hashtag days:** cheap to join (#ScreenshotSaturday on both X and Bluesky, #WishlistWednesday on X). Expect tens of wishlists, not hundreds, consistent with Zukowski's "about 20 per viral moment". Their main value is developer-network visibility, press and creator scouting, and asset iteration.
- **Free curated showcases and themed Steam fests:** the recurring events with the best evidence. For a survivors-like or roguelite, watch for a matching Steam themed fest; none is confirmed in the sourced list (see Gaps).
- **"Indie Sunday":** the name I know is a Reddit thread, which is out of scope. I found no non-Reddit "Indie Sunday" event.

### Gaps
- No source confirms a 2026–27 Valve roguelite/roguelike or survivors-like themed fest. Only Auto-Battler RPG, Shop Keeper and Couch Co-Op appeared in the sourced list.
- No wishlist measurements for any hashtag event.
- No verified details on #IndieDevHour or "Wholesome Direct"-style recurring X showcases beyond Wholesome Snack's deadline.

## What are the risks (bans, backlash, "AI" accusations) and best practices?

### Takeaway
**Main risks:**
- Being labeled advertising and downvoted or banned (Imgur, itch.io forums, Discord general chats).
- Generative-AI backlash, which can hit even games with large wishlists and has cost awards.
- Being cloned by AI or asset-flip copycats after going viral before having a Steam page.
- Platform dependency (X, Discord, Bluesky can all change).

**Best practices:**
- Use designated promo channels at milestones only.
- Lead with native GIF or visual content rather than "here's my game" titles.
- Avoid or clearly disclose generative AI.
- Tag every link with Steam UTM parameters.
- Funnel attention into owned channels (email list, your own Discord).

### Cited Findings
- **AI backlash:**
  - [Press, 2026; full article not retrievable, details from search excerpt] *Vapor World: Over The Mind* (Soulslike Metroidvania, Alive Inc.) reportedly had about 100,000 wishlists before its Aug 19 early-access launch. It drew backlash after swapping its in-engine cutscenes for AI-generated ones. Source: [PC Gamer – Steam Week in Review](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-a-touch-of-ai-is-all-it-takes-to-trigger-backlash-as-a-promising-new-indie-falls-afoul-of-slop-skeptics/)
  - [Press] Paolo Pedercini removed AI-generated voice lines from *Future?* after criticism he attributed to a Bluesky user. Source: [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/game-development/i-drank-from-the-chalice-of-blood-indie-dev-scrubs-his-game-of-ai-generated-voice-lines-having-previously-described-the-technology-as-anti-human-and-anti-art/)
  - [Press] The Indie Game Awards pulled two awards from Clair Obscur over generative AI use ("We have a hard stance against gen AI in videogames"). Source: [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/indie-game-awards-pulls-two-awards-from-clair-obscur-over-generative-ai-use-we-have-a-hard-stance-against-gen-ai-in-videogames/)
- **Cloning and AI-copy threats:**
  - A studio said its game went viral on social media before it had a Steam page, and several identical clones followed. Source: [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/entire-games-are-being-ripped-off-by-ai-and-things-are-only-going-to-get-worse/)

Source: [HTMAG – How to use Steam's UTM feature](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/04/14/how-to-use-steams-utm-feature-to-track-the-number-of-wishlists-and-sales-your-marketing-is-generating/)
- **Aggregator selection:** pick accounts by audience fit, not follower count, and try free placement first. Source: [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/08/07/how-to-get-wishlists-using-twitter-game-aggregators/)

Source: [GameTyrant](https://gametyrant.com/news/social-media-as-a-wishlist-building-tool-what-works-and-what-doesnt)

### Inferences
- **Highest reputational risk:** AI-generated art, capsules, trailers or voice lines. Bluesky and indie-award communities are especially hostile, and a single accusation can dominate the conversation around a game. Use original art and be prepared to show process (WIP shots, timelapses).
- **Cloning risk:** a Steam page should be live before any viral push, so that attention converts to wishlists and the developer's claim to the idea is established.
- **Practical cadence:**
  - Weekly hashtag posts (#ScreenshotSaturday on Bluesky and X).
  - Milestone-only posts in genre-fit Discords.
  - One well-crafted Imgur or HN post per major beat.
  - Everything UTM-tagged so the user can cut channels that yield under about 20 wishlists.
